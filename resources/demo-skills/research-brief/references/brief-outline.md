# Research Brief Outline

Use this outline for decision memos, market scans, vendor comparisons, and executive research summaries.

## Sections

1. Objective: one sentence naming the decision, audience, and deadline.
2. Context: the minimum background required to understand the brief.
3. Findings: facts and evidence grouped by theme.
4. Options: realistic paths forward with tradeoffs.
5. Risks: open questions, weak evidence, and known constraints.
6. Recommendation: a concise next action with rationale.

## Evidence Rules

- Prefer primary sources, recent documents, and user-provided material.
- Quote sparingly and only when exact wording matters.
- Keep inference visibly separate from source-backed facts.
